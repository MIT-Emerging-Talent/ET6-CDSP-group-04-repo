## Full Script

**Slide 1 – Title**  
Hello everyone, we are **SkyGen, Group 4**, and today we’ll share our project on career pathways for displaced youth  
entering IT jobs.  

**Slide 2 – Meet Amir**  
Meet Amir. Amir is 21, displaced, with no degree or U.S. credentials. Still, like many ambitious youth, he wants to  
break into tech quickly and affordably. He represents thousands facing the same challenge.  

**Slide 3 – Central Problem**  
Our question was: *How can displaced youth like Amir access IT jobs within 12 months?*  
This is about more than training — it’s about survival and opportunity. Many youth cannot afford long, costly degrees.  
We set out to find practical, scalable pathways that could help them enter the workforce quickly while building  
lasting careers.  

**Slide 4 – Data Challenges**  
Two big challenges: fragmented data across sources, and unclear, self-reported outcomes.  
Our pivot was creating a unified, person-level dataset linking barriers, education, and employment.  

**Slide 5 – Pathways & Barriers**  
What did we find?  

- Bootcamps: 78% employment, 41% salary growth  
- CS degrees: standard but costly and slow  
- Online & nonprofits: weaker results, more data needed  
Barriers like **cost, ESL, and documentation** heavily affect access.  

**Slide 6 – Sharing Insights**  
We aimed for clarity and empathy. We built visuals, a webpage, and Instagram posts, and engaged displaced youth  
directly through WhatsApp.
