# Milestone 3.1 — Exploratory Data Analysis and Sub-question Division

As part of Milestone 3.1, we divided our main research question — documented in the file `0_domain_study` — into
smaller, manageable sub-questions. This division was based on the background information and research we had gathered earlier.

Since our team is working asynchronously and split into five groups, each group took responsibility for exploring one of
these sub-questions:

1. **Barriers and Demographics** — Khadija Ramalwi
2. **Entry-Level IT Opportunities** — Shaima Mohamed
3. **Education Models and Accessibility** — Yuri Spizhovyi
4. **Employment Outcomes by Pathway** — Yevheniia Rudenko
5. **Employer Perceptions and Success Factors** — Olubusayo Solola (Simi) and Nelson Fodjo

We continued our work asynchronously, and all teams saved their findings and analyses in the folder `4_data_analysis`,
following the naming convention to keep the work organized and reproducible.

This approach allows us to comprehensively explore different aspects of the main question in parallel and ensures that
the subsequent synthesis of insights will be well-informed and structured.
